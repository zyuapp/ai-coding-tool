Start a task — dressing the empty Work view

Same parts, same behaviour, nothing added: glyph, title, one line of copy, the project / branch / worktree row, the composer, the session panel. Only their proportion, weight and grouping change — plus the Chat / Work switch, which today is a small dark pill floating with nothing to sit on. Four dressings, then the switch on its own.

1 — Composed

recommended

The block drops from the middle of the window to sit a fixed distance above the composer, so hero → controls → input read as one column instead of two islands. Glyph gets a bordered tile so it has the same material as the composer. Title goes to 25px at full ink, copy narrows and quiets, so the two stop competing. The three controls become one track — they were always one decision — with hairlines instead of gaps. Behind it all, a radial wash a few percent above the canvas, which stops the void reading as flat black.

2 — Plate

most literal

The middle gets a surface of its own and docks onto the composer — one plate, gradient fading into the canvas at the top, the controls sitting under a hairline in its footer. The seam radii are 6px on both sides so the two pieces read as one card that has been cut, not two boxes stacked.

3 — Quiet

no new boxes

The other direction: remove weight rather than add it. Nothing gains a border. The title carries the screen at 27px with tight tracking, the copy narrows to a 340px measure so it breaks in two even lines, and the controls become plain text with hairline separators that only ink up on hover. Chat / Work becomes two tabs on a rule that spans the window, which gives the pill something to sit on instead of floating.

4 — Ledger

left-aligned

Everything stays, but the block goes left-aligned to the composer's own edge, glyph beside the title rather than over it. The eye already starts at the left edge of the input, so the copy, the controls and the caret all sit on one axis. Cheapest to build of the four — it is a flex direction and some margins.
